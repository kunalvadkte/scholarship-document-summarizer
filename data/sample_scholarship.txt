About the Scholarship
---------------------
The ABC Education Foundation Scholarship 2026 is a merit-cum-means scholarship
offered by the ABC Education Foundation to support undergraduate students from
economically weaker sections of society. The scholarship aims to encourage
academically talented students to pursue higher education without financial
constraints.

Eligibility Criteria
--------------------
Applicants must satisfy all of the following conditions to be eligible for
this scholarship:

1. The applicant must be currently pursuing an undergraduate degree programme
   (B.Tech / B.E. / B.Sc. / B.Com / B.A.) from a recognised university or college.

2. Applicants must have obtained a minimum of 75% marks in their most recent
   qualifying examination. Applicants with CGPA-based grading must have
   achieved a CGPA of 7.5 or above on a 10-point scale.

3. The annual family income of the applicant must not exceed Rs. 4,00,000
   (Rupees Four Lakhs only) per annum from all sources.

4. Applicants belonging to SC, ST, OBC, and EWS categories are especially
   encouraged to apply.

5. Age: The applicant must be between 17 and 25 years of age as of the
   date of application.

Scholarship Benefits
--------------------
Selected students will receive a scholarship of Rs. 50,000 (Rupees Fifty
Thousand only) per year. The scholarship amount will be credited directly to
the student's bank account through NEFT/RTGS transfer.

In addition to the financial award, selected scholars will receive:
- Career counselling sessions
- Access to the foundation's online learning resources
- Certificate of merit from the ABC Education Foundation

Required Documents
------------------
Applicants must submit the following documents at the time of application:

1. Aadhaar Card (self-attested photocopy)
2. Previous Year Marksheet (self-attested photocopy)
3. Income Certificate issued by a competent authority
4. Caste Certificate (applicable for SC/ST/OBC applicants)
5. Bonafide Certificate from the current institution
6. Bank Account Details (cancelled cheque or first page of passbook)
7. Passport Photograph (recent, coloured, white background)
8. Domicile Certificate
9. College ID Card

All documents must be uploaded in PDF or JPEG format, with each file not
exceeding 2 MB in size.

Application Process
-------------------
Eligible students must apply online through the official application portal
of the ABC Education Foundation at www.abceducationfoundation.example.org.

Steps to apply:
1. Visit the official website and click on "Apply for Scholarship 2026".
2. Register using your email address and mobile number.
3. Complete the online application form with accurate personal and academic details.
4. Upload all required documents as specified above.
5. Submit the application before the last date.

Applicants are advised to keep a printed copy of the submitted application form
for future reference.

Selection Process
-----------------
Shortlisted candidates will be selected based on academic merit and financial
need. The selection committee may conduct a telephonic or online interview for
final selection. The decision of the ABC Education Foundation will be final
and binding.
